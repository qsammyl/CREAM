Credence·About

A diversified holding,
anchored in integrity.

Credence Real Estate & Asset Management is a premium real estate and property solutions company operating under Credence Holdings Global — with operations in the USA, the United Kingdom, and Ghana.

Our Story

Significance,
not size.

We began with a commitment to credibility. In markets where trust defines success, we chose to build enterprises anchored in integrity, governance, and measurable value creation. That commitment remains the foundation of everything we do today.

Beyond serving the diaspora community, we work closely with high-net-worth individuals, investors, and homeowners seeking premium development and property management services. Our team is committed to creating exceptional residential and commercial spaces that combine luxury, functionality, and long-term value.

At Credence, we understand that building a property is more than construction — it is about creating legacy, preserving wealth, and delivering comfort. This is why we manage every project with attention to detail, integrity, and excellence.

Whether you are abroad looking to build securely in Ghana, an investor seeking reliable property management, or a local client pursuing your dream home — Credence is your trusted partner every step of the way.

Vision & Mission

Two statements,
one direction.

A clear vision keeps the firm pointed forward; a disciplined mission keeps the team accountable to the clients in front of us today.
Our Vision

To become one of Africa's most trusted global real estate brands.

Connecting the diaspora and local communities to exceptional property opportunities in Ghana — by setting the standard for transparency, quality, and stewardship in the markets we serve.

Our Mission

To deliver world-class real estate, construction & asset management — with integrity.

Through innovation, professionalism, and integrity — helping clients build lasting value and legacy across residential and commercial property in Ghana and beyond.

Chairman / CEO Message

Our journey has never been
about size — it has always
been about significance.

We began with a commitment to credibility. In markets where trust defines success, we chose to build enterprises anchored in integrity, governance, and measurable value creation. That commitment remains the foundation of everything we do today.

As a diversified global holding company, we operate across financial services, real estate, consultancy, and lifestyle enterprises with a disciplined approach to growth. Each subsidiary is structured for performance, accountability, and long-term sustainability.

Our ambition is clear: to build institutions that outlive trends and create generational impact.

We believe strong leadership demands vision, but also responsibility — to clients, partners, employees, and the communities we serve. Growth without structure is noise. Growth with governance is legacy.

As we expand our global footprint, we remain guided by excellence, innovation, and an unwavering commitment to delivering value. To our partners and stakeholders, thank you for your trust. To future collaborators, we welcome strategic conversations.

Adeline Quarshie
Chief Executive Officer · Credence Holdings Global
