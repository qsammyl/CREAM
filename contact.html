Common Questions

What to ask
on a first call.

A few of the questions clients ask most often, with our short answers. Bring more on the call — we welcome them.

Do you work with diaspora buyers?

Yes — it's our specialism. We provide end-to-end service from land due diligence and title verification through construction, interior finishing, and ongoing management for clients in the USA, UK, and beyond.

How are properties verified?

Every property we list has been title-searched at Lands Commission, independently surveyed, and signed off by retained counsel. We share full documentation on confirmed enquiry.

Can I tour a property remotely?

Absolutely. We produce live walkthroughs via WhatsApp video, full cinematic tours for shortlisted homes, and arrange in-person tours for visiting family or counsel on your behalf.

What does property management include?

Tenant sourcing & vetting, rent collection, 24-hour maintenance, monthly statements, quarterly condition reports, and a dedicated account manager who knows your property by name.

Do you build on land I already own?

Yes. We provide standalone construction & project management for clients with existing land — from concept through handover, with weekly site reporting throughout.

What payment terms do you offer?

Most of our developments and services use a staged-payment schedule tied to verifiable milestones. We can discuss escrow arrangements with diaspora clients on request.
