Credence Real Estate and Asset Management (USA–Ghana) Launches Global Opportunities to Own a Home in Ghana

2023-01-07 · By Credence Holdings

The Credence brand has penetrated the real estate industry with global opportunities and well-researched, developed strategies to own a home — through its parent company Credence Holdings, USA, and Credence Real Estate and Asset Management, Ghana.

While properties in Accra are increasing in value and price, investing in real estate in Ghana is relatively more affordable than in more developed countries around the globe. For example, according to realtor.com, a credible rent site in America, it is estimated that the average American family pays about $1,827.00 per month for a one- or two-bedroom apartment. That is an equivalent of GH₵15,529.50 at the rate of 8.5 cedis to a dollar — an amount that could cater for about a year or two of advance payment for a two-bedroom apartment in some areas in Accra.

That notwithstanding, Ghana still has a large deficit in the housing industry and, consequently, a high demand for housing. Experts suggest that Ghana’s housing deficit stands at around 2 million. From an investor’s or individual’s point of view, this deficit creates opportunities to invest in housing in Ghana, especially in the cosmopolitan and metropolitan cities — namely Accra, Kumasi and Takoradi.

The rental market in Accra, for example, is vast. Statistics show that 37.5% of all households in Accra are renting — that is roughly 22% of the population of Ghana. Building a house in Ghana, as an individual or investor, could open up a new revenue stream for you from renting it out in the low, mid, or high-end market, as well as help you save money.

The opportunities in the real estate industry are quite mouth-watering, yet the challenges that face individuals are staggering. For example, getting a plot of land comes with some unassumed challenges and struggles that can only be noticed when one is deeply involved. Challenges such as litigation, threats on life, and the risk of being duped are but a few of them.

The current economic downturn of Ghana — with an inflation rate standing at 33.9% as at Aug. 2022, the cedi which had already depreciated in value by about 30.1% as at Jan. 2023 to the dollar on the interbank and forex markets, and the Bank of Ghana raising its monetary policy rate to 27% per annum in November 2022 — presents a few of the macro-economic challenges that stare in the face of any “newbie” who wishes to be a property owner, or any existing property owner who wishes to rent out or sell a property.

These challenges used to be a joy-stopper for persons interested in owning properties and making a business out of it — but not until Credence Real Estate and Asset Management, a subsidiary of Credence Holdings, came on the scene. With exceptional service and credibility, Credence has continuously impacted households and individuals and caused people to acquire properties with ease. The management of Credence Real Estate and Asset Management believes that everyone should be able to acquire properties with the assistance it has carved out for its customers.

Ms. Adeline Quarshie, at a launch of Credence Real Estate and Asset Management in the USA on Saturday, January 7, 2023, stated that “the company will help you build, offering you loans and flexibility in credibility. We have put together the simplest engineered method in research around the globe to help you own your house and home. No matter where you are in the world, count on Credence.”
