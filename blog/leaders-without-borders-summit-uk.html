Leaders Without Borders Summit Held in the UK

2023-05-19 · By Credence Holdings

Dated 17th to 19th May, 2023, and hosted at the Millennium Gloucester Hotel, Kensington, London, U.K, was the Leaders Without Borders Summit with the theme “Global Partnerships and Sustainable Investments” — with the sole aim of offering lively conversations and generating rich networking opportunities while providing learning opportunities in leadership, entrepreneurship, and respect for culture.

The LWB Annual Business Summit is not just a summit but a global initiative created to encourage a strong strategic economic and geopolitical partnership by promoting trade and investment. The LWB’s drive is global investment opportunities and development for stakeholders across borders.

Once again, Credence Holdings’ quest to provide tailored solutions to its cherished customers had already inspired us to birth and niche the “Credence Dream Home” and the “Credence Loan Abroad” products respectively. A product that has been designed for Ghanaians in the diaspora won Credence Holdings the prestigious award in the category of “Innovative Company of the Year” for the product Credence Dream Home for the diasporan community, and the Chief Executive Officer, Adeline Quarshie (Ms.), won for herself the Hall of Fame Woman of the Year Award at the Leaders Without Borders.

This leap cannot be emphasized enough given the fact that the Credence Dream Home and Loan Abroad is just one year after its introduction, yet could brew such impact with such intensity and be recognized by a prestigious international award body such as Leaders Without Borders Global.

This milestone establishes the drive of Credence Holdings’ bid to provide nothing but the best of products to serve its clientele. Credence observed with great care and concern the struggles of especially Ghanaians in the diaspora in their quest to build or acquire properties back home, and also non-Ghanaians who wish to create multiple streams of income for themselves. The financing ecosystem in Ghana has continued to develop over the past ten years, making it much more feasible for people to buy and access property.

That notwithstanding, though there are mortgage options available to anyone who wants to build or purchase properties in Ghana, the credit credentials as required have deterred many, especially those in the diaspora, from accessing them. Either the bureaucratic processes are too many, or one does not meet up with the creditworthiness scale of the said institution. Now, this is where Credence Holdings comes in to give better options with its brand-new product, “Credence Dream Home Loan.”

Quite beyond that, the recent expansion in the real estate industry is a sumptuous opportunity for anyone with the sole purpose of owning a property and creating multiple streams of income to jump into. The rental market in Accra, for example, is vast. Statistics show that 37.5% of all households in Accra are renting — that is roughly 22% of the population of Ghana.

Unfortunately, regardless of the fact that there are many opportunities lying in wait in the real estate industry, macro-economic conditions such as the depreciation of the cedi to the main trading currency, the dollar, and other factors such as the inflation rate and the policy rate, have made these opportunities almost inaccessible.

To mention but a few, the recent decline in trust in both family and friends alike has made it almost impossible for anyone in the diaspora to undertake a project back home, leaving it in the care of such ones in their absence. Some people have ended up being swindled, duped, or even scammed.

The challenge of acquiring litigation-free land, and even getting the land in a preferred area vis-à-vis its related cost, sometimes discourages many who want to undertake such projects back home. Others who tend to fall on building agents and even companies end up being charged sometimes double or thrice the amount the project would have cost.

What is the Credence Dream Home product? It is a carefully tailored product designed for both Ghanaians and non-Ghanaians in the diaspora who wish to build in comfort and also invest in the real estate industry in Ghana. Being aware of the challenges that mire the opportunities in real estate, Credence developed a staged model that would give especially those in the diaspora the easiness of building.

The stages are:

  1. Sub Structure
  2. Super Structure
  3. Shell Stage
  4. Finishing Stage

Other benefits that await are:

Providing Financial Support for the Project

Even though there seem to be mortgage plans, the conditions of some people in the diaspora are such that the mortgage plans do not feature their considerations, hence casting them out of access. However, the Credence Dream Home product affords you the grace to plan your finances and build at your own financial pace based on the structural stages demarcated in the project’s strategic strata levels.

Supervising Your Building Project Whilst You Focus on Your Daily Business

Credence Real Estate understands the dynamics of family and family goals in an ever-changing culture such as ours. People are becoming more and more nuclear in their family orientation, hence not wishing to allow extended family to come into the knowledge of important projects such as building, thereby necessitating the need to quietly and privately undertake these projects. Credence Real Estate is willing to do such supervision for you.

Beyond building for you, we are also master stewards in the business of managing assets — by renting, selling, or creating a market-attractive value for the property.

In all, it should be said the two-day summit was characterized by applause, appreciation, and jaw-dropping moments when Ms. Quarshie, the Chief Executive Officer, was given a fifteen-minute slot to speak to the audience on the products.

Everyone thinks Credence Dream Home and Loan Abroad is a carefully tailored product that would save many hearts from breaking and many pockets from being duped and scammed — and we at Credence think so too.

Taking the opportunity of the moment, Ms. Quarshie expressed her sincerest appreciation to the leadership of the Leaders Without Borders for the keen recognition of the product. She also thanked the entire customer base of Credence Holdings, the staff, management, media partners, and all and sundry. This award is dedicated to you all.

Credence Holdings. Still, we are credible…

Credibility in World Class Service.
